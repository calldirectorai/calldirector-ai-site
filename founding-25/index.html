Questions

Before you claim a spot

What do I need to send?

A photo, your logo if you have one, your services and starting prices, your phone number, your booking link if you use one, and a few lines about you. The intake form walks you through it.

Do I need my own domain?

If you have one, we use it. If you don't, we can buy one for you and bill it at cost.

Can I make changes?

One round of changes is included before launch. Send them together within 7 days of seeing your page. After launch, one small change a month is included. Bigger changes are quoted first.

Is the AI receptionist included?

No. The Launch Page includes your page, consultation form, VIP list, booking link, review links and hosting. The AI receptionist, chat assistant, missed-call text-back and social media are add-ons you can turn on anytime.

How does the VIP list work?

Visitors join from your page with their name, phone and email, and you pick the perk, like an early-bird discount. Each one gets a welcome email and you get an alert. When your booking opens, we send your whole list one message so they book first.

When am I billed?

You pay the $297 setup at checkout. Your $49 monthly plan starts only after your page is live, so you never pay monthly for a page you can't use yet.

Is there a contract?

No. Plans are month-to-month.

Why only 25 spots?

Every page is built by hand, not churned out. Capping the founding price keeps launch times fast for everyone who claims a spot.

Should I send client information here?

No. Never send Social Security numbers, tax documents or client details through this page.
