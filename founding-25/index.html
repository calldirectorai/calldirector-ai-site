Good to know

What a landing page is, and what it isn't

A landing page is

  • One page about you and your services.
  • Made to share: Facebook, Instagram, text messages, business cards and QR codes.
  • A simple way for clients to request a consultation or join your VIP list.

A landing page is not

  • A full website with many pages or a blog.
  • Built to show up high on Google searches. People find it through the links you share.
  • A place to upload tax documents. Keep using your secure method for those.
