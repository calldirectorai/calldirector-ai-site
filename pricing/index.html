Pricing Questions

Why is the Growth System custom priced?

Businesses differ in how customers contact them, how calls should be handled, which information matters, and what follow-up or integrations are needed. We scope the first phase around the gaps that matter most instead of forcing every business into the same package.

Do I need the full system to get started?

No. Some businesses start with Vera as a 24/7 AI front desk, while others begin with missed-call response, booking, or follow-up. The Lead Leak Audit helps identify the most practical starting point.

What happens after implementation?

After the system is live, the managed plan covers ongoing work to maintain and improve the configured system. Exact scope is confirmed during the planning process.

Is there a long-term contract?

The implementation fee covers building and launching the selected system. After that, managed service runs month-to-month with 30 days' notice to cancel.
